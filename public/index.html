Tessera

A serverless, peer-to-peer recreation of the grid territory game Empire Attack.

Play → The current client. Local play against bots, on a pannable, zoomable map. Original prototype → Frozen reference build. One human, one bot, on a fixed grid.

Empire Attack was conceived by Ian Andrew, jointly designed with Jon Grove who programmed it, and ran from 2008 to 2017. Tessera is an independent recreation, not affiliated with or endorsed by its creators. · Source
